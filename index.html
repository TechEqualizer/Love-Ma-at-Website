About

Meet Your Love Architect

I'm Dr. Love Ma'at — celebrity intimacy coach, speaker, author, producer and media personality.

For more than 30 years, I have studied and experienced love, sex, relationships, spirituality, pleasure, and the way people connect. I speak about the questions grown people have but are sometimes too embarrassed, ashamed, or afraid to ask.

My conversations are honest. They are humorous. And they are always free of judgment.

Through Love Boot Camp 365, Mysteries of Sex, and When Love Calls, I help people understand that intimacy is not limited to the bedroom — I help them explore who they are, communicate what they need, and become honest enough to let somebody else truly know them.

"I'm not here to teach people how to perform love. I help them become honest enough to experience it."
